Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

On September 12, 2022, Patrick Spence, Chief Executive Officer of Sonos, Inc. (“Sonos”), and Eddie Lazarus, Interim Chief Financial Officer and Chief Legal Officer of Sonos, will participate in a fireside chat beginning at 3:00 pm Pacific, as well as host investor meetings, as part of the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference.

 

During the conference, Sonos intends to reaffirm the fiscal year 2022 guidance issued at the time of its third quarter fiscal 2022 results. A live webcast and replay of the fireside chat will be accessible in the News & Events section of the Sonos investor relations website: https://investors.sonos.com/news-and-events/default.aspx#section=events-and-presentations.


The information contained in this Item 7.01 of Current Report on Form 8-K is being furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, regardless of any general incorporation language in such filing.
