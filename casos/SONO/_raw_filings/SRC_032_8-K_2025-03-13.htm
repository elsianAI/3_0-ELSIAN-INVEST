Item 5.07    Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The Annual Meeting of Stockholders of Sonos, Inc. (the “Company”) was held on March 11, 2025 (the “Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, there were present, in person or by proxy, holders of 108,908,780 shares of the Company's common stock, or approximately 90.35% of the total outstanding shares eligible to be voted. The holders present voted on the seven proposals presented at the Annual Meeting as follows.

Proposal One — Election of Directors

The Company’s stockholders approved the election of three Class I directors to the Company’s Board of Directors for three year terms or until their respective successors are elected and qualified or until their earlier resignation or removal, by the following votes:
