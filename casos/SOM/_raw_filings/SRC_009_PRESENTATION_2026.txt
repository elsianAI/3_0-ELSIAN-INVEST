Notice of Investor Presentation
Somero® is pleased to announce that, as part of its engagement with investors, following the release of its full-year results announcement on 10 March 2026, management will host a live virtual presentation and Q&A on 17 March 2026 at 1715 GMT.
The presentation is open to all existing and potential shareholders and will be given by Chief Executive Officer, Tim Averkamp, and Chief Financial Officer, Enzo LiCausi.
To register to attend, please use the following link:
https://www.investormeetcompany.com/somero-enterprise-inc/register-investor
Questions can be submitted at any time during the live presentation.
A recording will be made available on the Group's website following the conclusion of the presentation.
