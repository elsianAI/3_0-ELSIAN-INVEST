Karoon Energy Ltd is an ASX listed international oil and gas exploration and production company with assets in Brazil, the United States of America and Australia

Karoon’s vision is to be a leading, independent international energy company that adapts to a dynamic world in an entrepreneurial and innovative way

Karoon’s purpose is to provide energy safely, reliably and responsibly, creating lasting benefits for all its stakeholders
